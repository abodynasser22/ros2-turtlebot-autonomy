Defence guide: the dynamics and the questions

R7021E project, group 6. For the talk on Thursday 29 October. Nothing here is read passively: you predict, then check.

How to use it, about 90 minutes in total
  1. Map (10 min). Link the keywords with a reason. Wrong links are fine, a thin map is the point.
  2. Dynamics (40 min). For each card write your prediction first, then reveal. Rate yourself honestly.
  3. Derive it (10 min). Compute the numbers yourself before you press the button.
  4. Questions (30 min). Answer aloud, then reveal. Repeat only the ones you rated red.

    6. On the day

    • Answer in this order: one sentence, the number, then what we do not know.
    • "We did not test that" is an acceptable answer. Say how you would test it.
    • Never say the wheels slipped. Never say the fit cuts across. Never say the turn was complete at the crossing.
    • Measured, calculated and read are different things. Say which one it is.
    • The video is the controller's view at the odometry pose. The numbers are the ground truth.
